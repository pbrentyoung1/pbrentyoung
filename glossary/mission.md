Mission is the shared purpose a church exists to pursue together. It turns calling into common work and gives people a reason to organize, decide, and act as one.

Mission is not a sentence on the wall, a tagline, or the calendar of everything you do. It is the reason underneath the activity, the answer to why you exist together that keeps the work from becoming a pile of unrelated efforts. Calling shapes it. Identity keeps it truthful. Vision shows where it could lead.

Mission is also the story while it is still at home. It is the version you author and curate, spoken inside your own walls in your own words. That story does not stay inside. It crosses into other people at the touchpoints, every place they encounter you, and there it becomes Brand, the perception they carry away. You can curate the story at home, but you cannot control the Brand it becomes. You can only shepherd it. Faithfulness to the first is the only real influence you have on the second.

## In business

Mission is the shared purpose that focuses a company's work and decisions. It explains why the organization exists beyond what it sells.

## In the church

Mission names the work God has given a church to pursue together. It is discovered in calling and identity, not chosen to sound impressive, and it gives the community something to organize around and move toward.

## What remains true

Mission is the story at home. Brand is that same story once it has left home. Steward the first faithfully so the second can be trusted.
