A clear invitation to take the next appropriate step.

## In business

The call to action moves a person toward trial, purchase, or commitment, and it is measured relentlessly.

## In the church

A faithful call to action respects agency and matches the trust already established. It invites; it never corners. A Call to Action requires a clear plan. We can't invite someone on a trip that we haven't planned.

## What remains true

The ask should never be larger than the relationship. See earning the ask.
