The discipline of making the meaning, reason, and next step easy to understand.

Clarity is not merely saying less. It is doing the work of deciding what matters, removing what competes with it, and arranging what remains so another person doesn't have to sort the message for themselves.

## In business

Clarity helps the right people quickly recognize what is being offered, why it matters, and what they should do next. It reduces hesitation without manufacturing urgency.

## In the church

Clarity is a form of hospitality. It respects limited attention, reduces unnecessary barriers, and helps a distracted or unfamiliar person take a faithful next step without needing insider knowledge.

## What remains true

If people have to organize the message before they can act on it, the communication isn't finished.
